Created `docs/adr/0001-add-verbose-flag.md` (short-form ADR, since this is a reversible internal-tool change):

- **Decision**: `--verbose` prints per-step diagnostics to **stderr**, keeping stdout pipe-able; no `-v` alias to reserve it for `--version`
- **Rejected alternative**: log-file approach — unnecessary overhead for a terminal-first internal tool
- **Honest downside**: diagnostic output will be inconsistent across subcommands at first
- **Revisit trigger**: promote to structured logging (`--log-file`, levels) if verbose output becomes the primary bug-report artifact or an external consumer appears
